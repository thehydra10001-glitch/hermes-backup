# ASOC Exam Sample Question Paper 3

**Source:** Sample ASOC (Amateur Station Operator's Certificate) exam for Indian amateur radio licensing.
**Time:** 2 hours (General Grade), 1 hour (Restricted Grade)
**Questions:** 100 (50 per Section), all equal marks

## Section A — Radio Theory & Electronics (Q1-50)

### Receiver Fundamentals
1. Parameter that determines lowest signal reception capability of a receiver → **Sensitivity**
2. TRF stands for → **Tuned Radio Frequency**
3. Superheterodyne: tuned to 1255 KHz, IF=455KHz. Local oscillator? → **1710 KHz** (1255+455)

### Basic Electronics
4. Kirchhoff's Voltage Law → **Algebraic sum of voltages in a loop is zero**
5. Material with poor conductivity → **Silicon** (semiconductor; others are conductors)
6. Magnet does NOT attract → **Aluminum**
7. Unit of resistance → **Ohm**
8. LDR stands for → **Light Dependent Resistor**
9. Three leads of a transistor → **Collector, Base, Emitter**
10. Ohm's Law: R = → **V/I**
11. Two capacitors in series → **Lower than the least of the two capacitors**
12. Unit of energy in electronics → **Joules**
13. HF band → **3 MHz to 30 MHz**

### Components & Circuits
14. NOT a directional antenna → **Whip antenna** (omnidirectional)
15. Unit of current → **Ampere**
16. Small inductance in parallel with large → **Lower** (parallel reduces total)
17. Three 150µF capacitors in series → **50 µF** (series: 1/Ct = 1/150 + 1/150 + 1/150)
18. Red-Violet-Orange-Silver resistor → **27 kΩ, 10%** (2=red, 7=violet, ×1k=orange, silver=±10%)
19. RMS stands for → **Root Mean Square**
20. Peak=150V AC, RMS value is → **Less than peak value**
21. Capacitor and inductor in series → **Tuned Circuit** (LC tank)
22. Domestic inverter → **Converts DC to AC**
23. 25mA in Amperes → **0.025 A**
24. 2mA through 100kΩ resistor → **200 volts** (V = 0.002 × 100000)
25. Gold/Silver/Brown tolerance → **5%, 10%, 1%**

### TV & Broadcast
26. India's TV encoding → **PAL**
27. Video bandwidth in Indian TV → **4.2 MHz**
28. Sky wave propagation uses → **Ionosphere**
29. Ionosphere does NOT have layer → **C**
30. Ionosphere layer that disappears at night → **D**
31. Area of silence in sky/ground wave → **Skip zone**
32. Frequencies below critical frequency → **Returned to earth**
33. Mobile uplink frequency vs downlink → **More than** (higher frequency for uplink)
34. AIR does NOT broadcast → **Pulse Modulation transmission**
35. Diode can be used as → **All of the above** (detector, mixer, rectification)

### Antennas & Propagation
36. Geostationary orbit distance → **36,786 km** (approx 36,000 km)
37. Antenna used for direction finding → **Loop antenna**
38. Transformer converts → **AC voltage into different AC voltage**
39. 10:1 transformer matching 200Ω → **2 Ω** (impedance ratio = turns² ratio = 100:1)
40. Transformer losses → **All of the above** (hysteresis, eddy current, copper)

### Modulation & Amplification
41. Digital modulation technique → **Phase Shift Keying**
42. Component that dissipates energy → **Resistor** (capacitor/inductor store energy)
43. Most common amplifying stage → **Common Emitter**
44. Transistor biasing purpose → **All of the above** (avoid thermal runaway, stable operation, stable Q-point)
45. Ideal ripple factor → **0**
46. Full wave rectifier uses → **2 diodes**
47. Voltage smoothing device → **Capacitor**
48. Audio modulation in TV → **Frequency modulation** (FM for audio, AM for video in Indian system)
49. NICAM technique → **For transmitting stereo audio**
50. Fax machine sends → **Analog data over telephone media**

## Section B — Regulations & Operating Procedures (Q51-100)

### Rules & Licensing (India)

52. Amateur forbidden to transmit → **All of the above** (business, entertainment/music, advertisements)
53. Logbook preservation period → **12 months**
54. Logbook must contain → **All of the above** (date/time, call signs, summary)

### Operating Procedures
55. Top priority signal → **Distress signal**

59. End of message in Morse → **AR**

### Frequencies & Standards
80. NOT a standard frequency signal → **1 MHz** (standard: 5, 10, 15 MHz)
81. General Grade license renewal fee → **Rs 100** (under new rules)

83. Restricted Grade max DC power (144-146 MHz) → **25 watts**

### Admin

## Key Exam Tips

### Frequently Tested Topics
- **Q-codes:** QTR (time), QSB (fading), QRQ/QR (speed up/slow down), QRA (name), QRV (ready), QRL (busy), QRT (stop)
- **Phonetics:** L=Lima, Y=Yankee, W=Whiskey, 3=Tango Tree, 9=Nine
- **Morse prosigns:** AA(End of line), AR(End of message), TU(Thank you), CQ(Calling any station), AS(Stand by), SOS(Save our souls)
- **Emission designations:** A1A=Morse, J3E=SSB, F3E=FM, C3F=Vestigial sideband TV
- **Power limits:** General 400W (HF), 50W (VHF); Restricted 100W (HF), 25W (VHF)
- **Resistor color code:** Black-Brown-Red-Orange-Yellow-Green-Blue-Violet-Grey-White (0-9)

### Math Quick Reference
- Capacitors series: 1/Ct = 1/C1 + 1/C2 + ...
- Capacitors parallel: Ct = C1 + C2 + ...
- Inductors series: Lt = L1 + L2 + ...
- Inductors parallel: 1/Lt = 1/L1 + 1/L2 + ...
- Ohm's Law: V = I × R
- Resistor color bands: Red(2)-Violet(7)-Orange(×1k)-Silver(±10%) = 27kΩ ±10%
- Transformer impedance ratio = turns ratio²
